## 💡 About POYALO

### 🗣️ What Does *"POYALO"* Mean?
In Malayalam, **"പോയാലോ?" (*Poyalo?*)** is that irresistible question you whisper to your closest friend when spontaneous wanderlust strikes. It translates loosely to:  
👉 **"Shall we just go?"**  
👉 **"Shall we hit the road?"**  
👉 **"Shall we take the leap?"**

It’s the universal spark before every legendary journey — spoken over midnight tea, at airport gates, or when staring at a cheap one-way flight ticket.

<br />

### 💥 The Anti-Spreadsheet Manifesto
Most travel apps suck because they treat wanderlust like corporate project management:
- ❌ **40-row Excel spreadsheets** that turn holidays into stressful sprint reviews.
- ❌ **Dry Wikipedia text walls** that read like an encyclopaedia entry from 1998.
- ❌ **Hallucinating AI bots** that casually suggest a 15-minute walk across a 300km desert highway.
- ❌ **Surprise border rejections** when travellers find out their passport needed 180 days of validity *at the boarding gate*.

**POYALO flips the entire paradigm upside down:**

| 🚫 The Traditional Way | ⚡ The POYALO Way |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Boring text descriptions** | **Multi-panel comic strips** with speech bubbles, action sound decals, and 4 personality tone dials (*Roast, Professor, Storyteller, Kid*). |
| **Hallucinated schedules** | **Deterministic reality checks** calculating real Haversine distance, travel velocity, and SunCalc golden-hour solar physics in <50ms. |
| **Static 2D tourist maps** | **Interactive 3D WebGL exploration** — scout landmarks, leap across Al Qasba canals, and collect heritage relics directly in your browser. |
| **Last-minute passport panic** | **Real-time 180-day passport meter & visa matrix** that stamps compliance directly onto your interactive boarding pass. |
| **Over-reliance on internet** | **Offline-first PWA** with built-in Web Audio synthesizers that work 35,000 feet in the air with zero cell reception. |

<br />

### 🎨 The Aesthetic: Neo-Brutalist Comic-Book Punk
POYALO isn't afraid of personality. It features:
- **Zero blurry drop shadows:** Only crisp, punchy, hard-offset elevation (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #111827`).
- **Heavy retro ink borders:** Deliberate, bold black framing (`2.5px solid #111827`).
- **Warm newsprint canvas:** `#FDFBF7` parchment background paired with high-voltage highlighter tones (`#1B3FFF`, `#FBBF24`, `#FF5722`).
- **Sound Decals & Speech Bubbles:** Cartoon-styled `"YALLA!"`, `"BOING!"`, `"SWOOSH!"`, and `"KAPOW!"` reactions with directional speech bubble tails.

## ✈️ Origin Story: Born at LAYOVER 2026

> *"200 students. One overnight build. Zero fixed problem statements. Build something around travel and demo it by sunrise."*

**POYALO** was born from scratch in a single sleepless, caffeine-fueled night by **two friends** during **[LAYOVER 2026](https://tinkerhub.github.io/layover/)** — an overnight travel hackathon organized by **[TinkerHub](https://tinkerhub.org)** and **[The Visa Guy](https://tinkerhub.github.io/layover/)** at **TinkerSpace Calicut, Kozhikode** (10–11 October 2026).

The challenge was completely open:  
> *"Make something around travel (useful, weird, or gloriously pointless) and demo it by sunrise."*

With a flight route theme running from **CCJ (Kozhikode) to DXB (Dubai)**, we teamed up, locked ourselves in from 6:00 PM to 9:30 AM, and asked each other: **"പോയാലോ?" (*"Shall we go for it?"*)**.

We didn't take first place or win the grand prize Dubai tickets — **we weren't first, but at least we gave it everything we had, and it's the heart, sweat, and effort that counts, right?** What started as an overnight sprint turned into an app we genuinely love and continue to build with care: pairing interactive comic lore, deterministic travel validation, and an in-browser 3D WebGL world.

### 1. 🤖 AI Multi-Day Planner & Deterministic Reality Check
- **Global Destination Support:** Craft custom multi-day plans for any city (*"3 days in Tokyo"*, *"Weekend in Paris"*, *"4 days in Rome"*, *"Dubai Heritage Trail"*).
- **Zero API Key Leakage:** Runs key-free on the client with zero bundled secrets. Connects to serverless edge proxies or executes entirely on the local deterministic engine.
- **SunCalc Solar Physics:** Calculates exact sunset golden-hour times for the destination's real latitude/longitude and highlights prime golden-hour photo spots.
- **1-Click Entry Verification:** One-tap border requirement checks tailored to your nationality.

### 2. 🎨 Graphic-Novel Comic Story Studio
- **Graphic Novel Visuals:** Hard black borders (`2.5px solid #111827`), neo-brutalist offset drop shadows (`4px 4px 0px #111827`), speech bubbles with directional tails, narrative caption boxes, and sound decals (`"SWOOSH!"`, `"YALLA!"`, `"KAPOW!"`).
- **4-Way Tone Dial:**
  - 🎓 **Professor:** Archival schematics, trade history, and rigorous chronological facts.
  - 🎬 **Storyteller:** Cinematic prose, dramatic noir lighting, and character-driven narrative.
  - 🔥 **Roast:** Witty satire exposing tourist traps, overhyped queues, and cultural ironies.
  - 🧒 **Kid:** Fun Saturday-morning cartoon strips with adventurous mascots.
- **Era Time Machine:** Shift between Ancient Heritage (1800s), Golden Age (1950s), and Modern Cyber eras to see how landmarks transformed over time.
- **Voice Narration & Web Audio Synth:** Real-time speech synthesis reads comic panels aloud with pitch modulation and retro sound effects.

### 3. 💡 Cultural Myth Pointer & Local Etiquette
- **Curated Myth Debunking:** Unpacks local myths vs realities across 17+ international cities (Dubai, Tokyo, Rome, Paris, Kozhikode, Amsterdam, London, Bangkok, New York, etc.).
- **Categorized Insight Chips:** Etiquette, financial norms, culinary customs, and safety rules.
- **"Suggest Myth to Admin" Modal:** Travelers can submit new local cultural quirks directly to the platform with local persistence.

### 4. 🎮 "VG World" 3D WebGL Exploration
- **In-Browser Three.js Engine:** Zero plugins, instant loading on mobile and desktop browsers with custom PCFSoftShadow tracking.
- **Mobile Multi-Touch Ergonomics:**
  - **Virtual Touch Joystick:** 128px outer base, 56px thumb target ($\ge 48\text{px}$ invariant), 8px dead zone, active glow scaling, and 4-way directional indicators (▲▼◀▶).
  - **Independent Jump Buffer:** 240ms jump buffer allows simultaneous running, turning, and jumping on phones with zero dropped inputs.
  - **Turbo Dash (`⚡ DASH`):** On-screen mobile sprint button (or `Shift` on desktop) providing a 3.5s 1.8x speed surge with cartoon sound effects.
- **Procedural Architectural Landmarks:**
  - *Burj Khalifa:* 6 tiered setback towers, cyan glowing LED balcony rings, entrance glass lobby, and pulsating red aircraft beacon.
  - *Dubai Frame:* Gold lattice diagonal crossbars and translucent cyan skybridge observation floor.
  - *Burj Al Arab:* Cantilevered circular helipad disk with yellow 'H' markings, exoskeleton truss arches, and causeway pier.
  - *Museum of the Future:* Landscaped green park hill podium and calligraphic window accent slits.
  - *Al Qasba Canal & Avenue:* Water canal with bridges, park walkways, and local characters (*Aadhu & Momo*).
  - *Bedouin Desert Camp:* Camel stalls, campfires, and terracotta carpets away from the urban skyline.
- **Proactive Landmark Discovery:** Approaching any landmark within 9.5m triggers celebration chimes, a comic discovery badge, and awards +150 XP towards your session score.
- **Zero VRAM Leak Invariant:** Comprehensive scene traversal with `.dispose()` calls on all geometries, textures, and materials during component unmount.

### 5. 🛂 POYALO Visa Concierge & Boarding Pass Studio
- **180-Day Passport Expiry Home Screen Widget:**
  - Evaluates the strict international 6-month passport validity invariant.
  - Visual color-coded validity meter, live countdown, and copyable advisory text.
  - One-tap sync with the Boarding Pass.
- **Interactive Boarding Pass Studio:**
  - Neo-brutalist flight card with mobile vertical tear line, circular punch notches, and barcode decals.
  - Real-time editable passenger name, origin, destination, seat, flight number, and date.
  - **Official 180-Day Immigration Security Seal:** Dynamically stamps `PASSPORT COMPLIANT (180+ DAYS)` with verified green badge.
- **Visa Compatibility Matcher:**
  - Cross-matches passport nationality and residency status into Visa-Free, Visa on Arrival, eVisa, or Embassy Required.
- **30-Second Visa Stress Quiz:**
  - Playful 5-step quiz measuring deadline pressure, missing documents, appointment availability, and itinerary complexity with actionable recommendations.

## 🎨 Design System & Neo-Brutalist Guidelines

POYALO adheres to strict visual rules defined in [`docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`](./docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md):

1. **No Blurry Soft Shadows:** Elevation is strictly expressed via hard flat offsets: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #111827`.
2. **Heavy Ink Outlines:** All borders are chunky and deliberate: `border-2` or `border-[2.5px] #111827`.
3. **Warm Newsprint Canvas:** High-contrast retro canvas with warm cream paper (`#FDFBF7`) and deep ink black (`#111827`).
4. **Smartphone-First Ergonomics:** All touch targets are $\ge 48\text{px}$. Boarding passes stack vertically with tear perforations on mobile screens (375px+).
5. **Comic Decals & Action Decals:** Speech bubbles with directional pointers, narrative captions, and sound decals (`WHOOSH!`, `BOUNCE!`, `ZOOM!`).

## 🚀 Local Development Setup

### Prerequisites
- [Bun](https://bun.sh/) (recommended) or Node.js 18+

### Quick Start

```bash
# 1. Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/nichroot/poyalo.git
cd poyalo

# 2. Install dependencies
bun install

# 3. Start development server
bun run dev
```

Open [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173) in your browser.

### Building for Production

```bash
# Typecheck and build optimized bundle
bun run build

# Preview production build locally
bun run preview
```

### Environment Variables (Optional)

The app works **100% offline** with built-in verified city databases and deterministic engines. To enable live Google Gemini AI calls:

Create a `.env.local` file:
```env
GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key_here
```
> **Security Note:** `GEMINI_API_KEY` is only accessed on the serverless proxy (`api/generate-plan.ts`). It is never exposed in client bundles. Users can also enter their own key in the in-app `ApiKeyModal` (stored exclusively in browser `localStorage`).

## 🔒 Privacy & Security Invariants

1. **Zero Passport Number Storage:** POYALO never prompts for, transmits, or stores sensitive identification numbers (passport numbers, national IDs). Only nationality and expiry dates are evaluated.
2. **API Key Isolation:** Server keys are strictly confined to serverless runtime functions. Client builds are verified to contain zero secrets.
3. **100% Offline Resilience:** The app remains completely operational on airplanes or remote travels using offline Service Worker caching, client-side rule validation, and Web Audio synthesizers.
