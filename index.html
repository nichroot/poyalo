POYALO (പോയാലോ?) — AI Travel Companion, 3D UAE World & Comic Lore

Offline-first worldwide travel application pairing interactive comic-book lore, deterministic itinerary validation, and 3D WebGL exploration.

Key Features

  • 3D WebGL World Exploration: Roam interactive 3D UAE landmarks including Museum of the Future, Dubai Creek, Bedouin desert camps, and Sharjah Al Qasba canal.
  • AI Travel Itinerary Planner: Deterministic reality check engine validating schedules, budget pacing, and 180-day passport & visa rules.
  • Comic Book Travel Lore: Visual narrative storytelling with comic panels, speech bubbles, local customs, and cultural heritage insights.
  • Travel Myth Buster: Instant local etiquette answers, debunking common tourism misconceptions.
  • Offline Boarding Pass: Generate and store offline-first travel achievement cards.
